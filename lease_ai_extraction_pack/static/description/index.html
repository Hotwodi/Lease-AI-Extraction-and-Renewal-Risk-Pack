Lease AI Extraction & Renewal Risk Pack

$499.99 USD

The Lease AI Extraction & Renewal Risk Pack is a comprehensive Odoo 18 module that leverages AI to streamline lease management workflows. It automates the extraction of critical lease data from uploaded documents, assesses tenant renewal risk with AI-driven probability scoring, tracks critical dates with proactive alerts, and enables side-by-side lease comparison analysis.

Key Features

  • AI-Powered Lease Extraction — Upload lease documents (leases, amendments, renewal letters, CAM statements) and let AI extract structured fields with confidence scores and page references.
  • Extracted Field Management — Review, verify, and correct AI-extracted fields across categories including dates, rent, escalation, deposits, renewal, CAM, clauses, and parties.
  • Renewal Risk Assessment — Evaluate tenant renewal probability with AI scoring, risk factor analysis, market rent comparison, and actionable recommendations (renew proactively, renegotiate, prepare for turnover, or monitor).
  • Critical Date Tracking — Monitor lease expiries, renewal notice deadlines, escalation dates, option deadlines, and rent reviews with automatic day-counting and AI priority scoring.
  • Lease Comparison Analysis — Compare two leases side-by-side across rent, terms, escalation, CAM, and deposits with AI favorability assessment and recommendations.

Document Types Supported

  • Lease agreements
  • Amendments
  • Renewal letters
  • CAM (Common Area Maintenance) statements
